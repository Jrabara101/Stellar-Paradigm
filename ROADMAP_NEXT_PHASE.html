Scramble Board — 2–4 player multiplayer

Shipped

Full 15×15 Scrabble-style tile game. Opponents are drawn from real wallets on the on-chain leaderboard — a deliberate stand-in for real player-vs-player, built so live peers can replace the AI later without a rebuild.

On-chain vocabulary credentials

Shipped

Scores and achievements post to a Soroban contract on Stellar testnet — verifiable, wallet-owned proof of skill instead of a database row only the app can see.

Next — Mainnet & Real Adoption

Target: ~2 weeks out

The highest-stakes phase: moving from testnet play-money to a real production app with real users. Everything else waits behind the first item.

Mainnet contract deployment

Blocking

Redeploy the leaderboard and reward contracts to Stellar mainnet; point the live app at them instead of testnet.

Blocked on: funding a mainnet Stellar account — covers deploy fees and becomes the fee-sponsorship source below. Nothing after this can start until it's funded.

Fee Sponsorship (gasless onboarding)

Scoped

New players get a sponsored account + fee-bumped transactions, so they can earn on-chain credentials without buying crypto first — the single biggest drop-off point for any Web3 game.

Why this over alternatives: chosen over multi-sig, cross-border (SEP-24/31), and account abstraction specifically because it directly unblocks new users, not because it's the flashiest option.

Security review

Scoped

Self-conducted contract review, written up and signed off before mainnet traffic — includes a fix already found: mint_badge currently has no caller restriction and needs one before deploy.

Launch marketing + 20 verified mainnet users

Scoped

Public launch post, demo content, and the user-verification pass repeated for mainnet (the testnet 51-user proof doesn't carry over — mainnet needs its own count).

Later — New Ways to Play

Post-mainnet

Already researched with real numbers, not just ideas — ordered by lift, cheapest first.

Speedrun mode

Scoped

Timed word-scramble runs on a time-bank formula. Reuses the existing word pool and scoring engine as-is — the only new surface is a clock.

Weekly Arena & Category Rush

Scoped

A recurring competitive window and a category-focused variant — both pure configuration on top of systems that already exist (leaderboard, categories).

Streak Freeze

Scoped

One miss no longer resets a win streak. Targets the most-cited complaint about Wordle-style streak systems: zero partial credit on a slip.

Scramble Board Duel

Scoped

A 2-player scoped-down version of the shipped 15×15 board, proving out real player-vs-player before attempting full 4-player parallel play.

Vision — Fully On-Chain

No committed date

Where the on-chain identity idea from the pitch goes if it's taken all the way.

On-chain seasons

Scoped

Daily leaderboard, streaks, and commit-reveal scoring framed as "Season 1" — needs one further contract redeploy.

Player profiles

Scoped

A persistent on-chain identity tying every credential, badge, and season result to one wallet.

Tradeable badge marketplace

Concept

Badges already mint on-chain via the reward contract — letting players trade or gift them turns a static reward into a real collectible, and doubles as a second on-chain feature.
