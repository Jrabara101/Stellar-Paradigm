# Founders Live — 99-Second Pitch Script
## Word Scramble Stellar

**Format:** 99 seconds spoken + 4 minutes audience Q&A. No live gameplay walkthrough — too risky in this
window (device switching / a glitch eats seconds you can't get back). Use ONE static screenshot or a
5-second pre-recorded clip on a slide behind you instead. Say the app is doing the work; don't drive it live.

## Script

**[0:00–0:12] HOOK**
> "Everyone's played a word game. Wordle, crossword, scramble puzzles. You get good, you feel smart for a
> day, and then... nothing. No proof it ever happened."

**[0:12–0:37] WHAT IT IS**
> "Word Scramble Stellar is a word game where your skill becomes something you actually own. Every score
> you set gets recorded on the Stellar blockchain — so your vocabulary achievements aren't trapped in one
> app's database, they're yours, verifiable, forever. And here's the part that usually kills Web3 games at
> the door: you don't need to own any crypto to play. We built fee sponsorship directly into the app — your
> first transaction is completely free, no wallet funding required."

**[0:37–0:57] PROOF / TRACTION**
> "This isn't a concept — it's live, on Stellar mainnet, right now. Real contracts, real players, real
> on-chain scores you can verify yourself on a block explorer. We already proved the model on testnet with
> over 50 verified users, and we're now onboarding real players onto mainnet with that same gasless
> experience."

**[0:57–1:22] ROADMAP**
> "Next: we're scaling mainnet adoption, and building out on-chain vocabulary credentials — soulbound proof
> that you actually mastered a subject, not just played a game. Something a resume can't fake, and a
> platform can't take away from you."

**[1:22–1:39] ASK / CLOSE**
> "Word Scramble Stellar — where being good at words means something that lasts. [Vote for us / We're
> looking for X — pick your actual ask]."

## Notes
- **Word count:** ~210 words total — comes in around 84-90 seconds at a natural 140-150 wpm pace, leaving a
  little room for your actual close line (the placeholder above is short on purpose). Practice out loud
  with a timer; trim the roadmap section first if you're running long, it's the most cuttable.
- **This script assumes mainnet + fee sponsorship are live** (both shipped 2026-08-20/21, confirmed working
  end-to-end on real user transactions as of 2026-08-23 — see `SECURITY.md` and the mainnet contract IDs in
  `README.md`). Don't revert to "we're planning to..." language — it's already true, say it that way.
- **Mainnet user count is still small and growing** (2 verified as of 2026-08-23, target 20+) — the script
  deliberately does NOT claim a specific mainnet user number, since it would likely be outdated by pitch day
  and a stale/wrong number undercuts credibility more than a true general claim. Keep the testnet 50+ number
  (still accurate, still real proof of the model) as the concrete number in the pitch, and only add a
  mainnet number live in Q&A once you know the current count.
- **Close line is a placeholder** — decide what you actually want from the room (an intro, feedback,
  mainnet funding lead, etc.) and swap it in. A concrete ask lands better than a generic "thank you."
- **Screenshots needed:** (1) Scramble Board gameplay, (2) leaderboard (ideally mainnet, once it has enough
  entries to look populated — testnet leaderboard is a fine fallback), (3) a simple one-line roadmap slide.
  Keep text on slides minimal — the audience should be listening to you, not reading past you.
